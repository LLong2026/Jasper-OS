# Arete — Legal Document Semantic Parsing

**Agent:** Arete
**Severity:** High
**Method:** fine_tuning
**Gap:** Legal Document Semantic Parsing

## Description
Parses legal and regulatory documents (patents, SBIR solicitations, compliance standards, GDPR/SOC2/PCI requirements) into structured compliance rules for automated enforcement.

## Capabilities
- Extract compliance obligations from regulatory text (GDPR, SOC2, PCI DSS, FedRAMP)
- Parse patent claims into structured claim trees for infringement analysis
- Extract SBIR/STTR solicitation requirements into proposal templates
- Generate CompliancePolicy records from parsed regulatory documents
- Map legal obligations to technical enforcement mechanisms
- Track compliance obligation changes and alert on updates

## Triggers
- New regulation needs compliance rule extraction
- Patent document requires claim parsing
- SBIR solicitation needs requirement extraction
- Compliance audit needs obligation mapping
- Regulatory update detected
