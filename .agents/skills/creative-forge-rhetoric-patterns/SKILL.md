# CreativeForge — Creative Rhetoric Patterns

**Agent:** CreativeForge
**Severity:** Low
**Method:** prompt_engineering
**Gap:** Creative Rhetoric Patterns

## Description
Advanced rhetorical technique library for generating persuasive, narrative-driven content across documentation, marketing materials, and technical papers.

## Capabilities
- Apply classical rhetoric (ethos, pathos, logos) to technical documentation
- Generate metaphor frameworks for complex technical concepts
- Structure narrative arcs for white papers and case studies
- Adapt tone and register for different audiences (technical, executive, regulatory)
- Apply persuasion patterns for investor and stakeholder communications

## Triggers
- White paper or technical paper needs narrative structure
- Marketing content requires persuasive framework
- Investor presentation needs rhetorical polish
- Technical concept needs metaphor-based explanation
